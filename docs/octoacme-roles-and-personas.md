# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction and organizational support for a project. They are accountable for high-level decisions that exceed the project team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and champion the intended outcomes
- Secure or approve funding, staffing, and priority when needed
- Resolve escalations that the PM and Product Manager cannot settle
- Review progress and make timely go/no-go or scope trade-off decisions

### Collaboration
- Partner with the PM on escalations, resources, and project health
- Align with the Product Manager on outcomes and priority decisions
- Consult stakeholders and delivery leads before making decisions that affect scope or commitments
- Receive concise status, risk, and decision updates from the PM

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements, workflows, and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business rules, user needs, and process requirements
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and impacts across workflows
- Help keep requirements and acceptance criteria clear and traceable

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to clarify scope, outcomes, and backlog items
- Coordinate with stakeholders and customer-facing roles to validate needs
- Review edge cases and requirements with Developers and QA/Testing
- Share requirement changes with the PM so plans, dependencies, and risks stay current

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide technical design and implementation coordination while helping the team manage technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and design decisions within agreed constraints
- Coordinate estimates, technical dependencies, and implementation approach
- Surface technical risks, trade-offs, and feasibility concerns early
- Support code quality, engineering standards, and technical review

### Collaboration
- Coordinate day-to-day technical work with Developers
- Work with the PM on sequencing, estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Partner with the Product Manager on feasibility and scope trade-offs
- Consult QA/Testing, Security/Privacy, and Release/Operations roles on quality and readiness

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX or Product Designers research user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and identify usability needs
- Create and maintain interaction designs, prototypes, and design guidance
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback
- Consider accessibility and consistency in the user experience

### Collaboration
- Align with the Product Manager on user outcomes and product direction
- Work with Developers and the Technical Lead to assess feasibility and implementation details
- Partner with QA/Testing to make experience expectations testable
- Share research findings and design decisions with stakeholders and the PM

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA or Test Leads coordinate the test approach and provide visibility into product quality and acceptance readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and acceptance validation with the team
- Coordinate test planning and report defects, quality risks, and test results
- Ensure test environments and required test data are ready
- Recommend release readiness based on agreed quality criteria

### Collaboration
- Partner with Developers and the Technical Lead on testability and defect resolution
- Align with the Product Manager on acceptance criteria and with the PM on test milestones and risks
- Validate designs and requirements with the Designer and Business Analyst
- Coordinate release verification with the Release/Operations Owner

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security or Privacy Leads advise on security and privacy requirements and help teams identify and address related risks.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and data-handling requirements
- Review designs and risks, and recommend proportionate mitigations
- Coordinate specialist review or incident escalation when needed
- Confirm that required security and privacy checks are addressed before release

### Collaboration
- Work with the Technical Lead and Developers to integrate safeguards into the design and implementation
- Coordinate with the Business Analyst and Product Manager on requirements and user-data implications
- Share risks and required actions with the PM for tracking and escalation
- Partner with QA/Testing and the Release/Operations Owner on verification and release controls

---

## Release / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Release or Operations Owners coordinate production readiness, deployment, and operational follow-through.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment plans, operational readiness, and required approvals
- Prepare monitoring, support handoffs, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Verify operational signals after deployment and coordinate incident response
- Capture operational follow-up actions and communicate service impacts

### Collaboration
- Coordinate timing and stakeholder communications with the PM
- Work with the Technical Lead and Developers on deployment dependencies and rollback plans
- Align with QA/Testing on smoke tests and release verification
- Prepare support teams with the Customer Support Representative

---

## Customer Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support Representatives contribute customer-impact knowledge and prepare support teams to assist users through changes.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, common issues, and support trends with the team
- Identify customer communication and support-readiness needs
- Prepare or update support guidance and escalation paths for releases
- Report post-release issues and recurring customer impacts

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to connect customer feedback to product priorities
- Coordinate with the Release/Operations Owner on release notes, support materials, and escalation readiness
- Share customer-impact risks with the PM and relevant stakeholders
- Help Developers and QA/Testing reproduce and prioritize reported issues

---

## Assigning Roles

Projects may combine roles based on their size and needs. The PM and Product Manager should make ownership explicit during planning, identify any specialist support required, and ensure that combining roles does not leave a responsibility or decision without an accountable owner.
